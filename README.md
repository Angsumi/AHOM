# Chronicles of the Ahom Kingdom (1228–1838 CE)

> **Live Interactive Timeline:** [https://angsumi.online/AHOM/](https://angsumi.online/AHOM/) *(also available at [https://angsumi.github.io/AHOM/](https://angsumi.github.io/AHOM/))*

An exhaustive, verified historical archive, visual timeline, and master dataset chronicling the 610-year reign of the Ahom Dynasty in Assam, India.

---

## 🏛️ Project Features

- **Interactive Visual Timeline (`index.html` / `timeline.html`):**
  - Standalone, high-performance web application designed for mobile and desktop.
  - Interactive era filtering across all 8 major historical periods.
  - Live full-text search across monarchs, battle titles, years, and historical figures.
  - Timeline vs. Grid layout switchers.
  - High-resolution modal viewer with complete event descriptions and historical context.
- **Master Dataset (`AHOM_KINGDOM_MASTER_CHRONOLOGY.csv`):**
  - 233 chronologically sorted rows with 19 detailed attributes per event/reign.
  - 100% historical accuracy with validated monarch names, dynasties, and dates.
- **Complete Artwork Collection (`images/`):**
  - 233 illustrations depicting the historic foundation, battles, architectural triumphs, and cultural milestones of the Ahom kingdom.

---

## 📜 Historical Eras Covered

1. **Foundational Era (1228–1497 CE):** Chaolung Sukaphaa's migration across Patkai, Charaideo establishment, and initial statehood.
2. **Imperial Expansion & Early Invasions (1497–1603 CE):** Suhungmung Swarganarayan, defeat of Turbak, Garhgaon capital.
3. **Mughal-Ahom Wars & Zenith (1603–1681 CE):** Pratap Singha's Paik reforms, Mir Jumla's invasion, and Lachit Borphukan's triumph at the Battle of Saraighat.
4. **Ministerial Intrigue & Terror (1672–1681 CE):** Debera Borbarua, Laluk Sola, and the martyrdom of Sati Joymoti.
5. **Tungkhungia Restoration & Golden Age (1681–1769 CE):** Gadadhar Singha, Rudra Singha, Rangpur, Talatal Ghar, Rang Ghar, and Kareng Ghar.
6. **Moamoria Rebellions & Decline (1769–1817 CE):** Religious conflict, peasant uprisings, Captain Welsh expedition, and Purnananda Burhagohain's regency.
7. **Burmese Invasions & Fall of Kingdom (1817–1826 CE):** Manor Din, devastation of Assam, and the Treaty of Yandabo.
8. **British Annexation & End of Monarchy (1833–1838 CE):** Tributary reign and deposition of Purandar Singha.
